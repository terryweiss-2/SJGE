# Game Economy

The game only supports an in-game currency called **credits**, although configuration
should drive the name of the currency that the user sees. Credits may be spent to improve the team by purchasing
improvements and player improvement plans. Credits are also needed to pay for players' salaries. The Manager may earn
credits through season performance, sponsorship, attendance and other means.

The game should not be a "pay to win" game. If anything it will be "pay to make it easier". Players may be traded
between teams and their salary has to be accounted for.

Some leagues will require a spending cap. Others will allow unlimited spending. But care should be taken that teams with
very large budgets do not play smaller teams regularly by defining leagues that support inclusion rules to define who is
allowed into a league. "regularly" is yet to be defined.

Budgets should be recalculated at the end of each game.
