# Game Leagues

Leagues are collections of teams that play each other on a schedule. Leagues define rules that determine the
teams that are allowed into a league. There are several types of leagues.

Amateur Leagues - teams are enrolled into these leagues automatically. If there are not enough humans available to fill
the league at the start of a season, teams will be created automatically to fill out the league. Teams should be evenly
distributed in terms of skill level and winning percentage. Payouts are good but not great

Pro Leagues - You have to earn your way into these leagues. A threshhold skill level and budget are required to join.
This will help prevent very powerful teams from overwhelming new players. Payouts as very good.

Major Leagues - These are the best players and managers. Payouts are very good, but the championship payout is huge.

Users cannot leave a league during a season. The team will play without them. But teams can change leagues between
seasons.

Different games may call this something other than these names, for instance in motorosport they will be called Atlantic
Championship, Lights Championship, World Championship, and the World Championship.
