# Services

The system will be expressed as a series of services that work together to provide a comprehensive, reliable and
flexible system.

## Components

Each component is a service that has its own lifetime and concurrency rules.

## Membership

This tracks the human players, their real currency balances and permissions and other standard information like first
and last, gamer tag and any other information that may be needed. Information that may be required by legislation is
also provided here. Theer will be no username/password structure, we will not store passwords. All logins must come
through Google, Micriosft or Apple.

## Transactions/Economy

This tracks the transactions between players and the system. It includes the amount of currency transferred, the time of
the transaction, the type of transaction and any other information that may be needed.

## Games

This engine is where games are played. It includes the game rules, the game state, the game results and any other
information that may be needed. All information from every game will be stored in the persistent store

## History

Statistics and analyitics for all players for all time.

# Leagues

League definition and team assignments.
