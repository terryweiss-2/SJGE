# Game Teams

Teams are organizations of players that have roles and responsibilities within the game. Managers assemble teams by
improving players from their original draft.

Teams are created by draft. Managers have a budget they can use to pay for players during the draft. Newly drafted teams
have their own budget and don't inherit anything from other teams they may own. This is the only way to create a team.

Retired teams can be added to new leagues and continue to play.

There should be a core of computer controlled teams that are owned by the game itself. These teams will be used to fill
out leagues with teams that are not owned by players. A core of these teams will have infinite life and attempt to win
championships. These teams will be identified as "core" teams and will try to compete in the major leagues. All other
teams will remain in their leagues even if they win a championship
